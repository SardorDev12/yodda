Yodda — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Yodda ("the app") is a spaced-repetition learning app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Short version

An account is required to use Yodda. When you create one, we store your email address and the learning content you add (books, units, cards, and review history) in a cloud database, so the same data stays in sync across every device you sign in on. This data is used solely to provide the app's core function and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Account & data storage

Creating an account (email address and password) is required before you can use the app. Once signed in:

  • We store your email address (for authentication) and the content you create — books, units, cards, review history, and app settings (language, theme, notification preferences) — associated with your account.
  • This data is kept in a cloud database and synced automatically to every device where you're signed in; it is not stored locally on your device outside of what your browser or OS caches while the app is open.
  • This data is used solely to provide the app's sync functionality and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us using the details below.

Backup / export feature

Yodda lets you export your data as a JSON file so you can keep a portable copy or move it to a different account. This file is created on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it yourself (for example, via email or a cloud drive you control). We never receive a copy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device using the operating system's notification service. No notification content or scheduling data is sent to us beyond the reminder preferences already described above.

Third-party services

Yodda uses Supabase to provide authentication and cloud data storage; your account email and learning content are processed by Supabase on our behalf under their own data processing terms. Yodda does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not share your data with any other third party.

Permissions

The app may request notification permission (for review reminders) and, when you use export/import, access to share or pick a file. These are used only for the features described above.

Children's privacy

Yodda is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing on their own behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your account and data, can be directed to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's Google Play store page.
