Yodda — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Yodda ("the app") is a spaced-repetition learning app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Short version

By default, Yodda does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data — everything you create stays on your device. Yodda also offers an optional account feature so you can sync your data between your own devices; this section explains what that involves if you choose to turn it on.

What data the app stores, and where

All content you add — books, units, cards, review history, and your settings (language, theme, notification preferences) — is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. By default none of it is sent to us or to any third party, and the app can be used fully without an account, login, or internet connection.

Optional account & sync

If you choose to create an account (email address and password) from the Settings screen, Yodda will sync your books, units, cards, and review history to a cloud database so the same data stays up to date across your devices. This is entirely optional and off by default — the app works normally without signing in. If you sign in:

  • We store your email address (for authentication) and the learning content described above, associated with your account.
  • This data is used solely to sync your content between your own devices and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • You can sign out at any time from Settings; your device keeps a local copy of your data regardless.
  • To request deletion of your account and synced data, contact us using the details below.

Backup / export feature

Yodda lets you export your data as a JSON file so you can move it to a new device. This file is created on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it yourself (for example, via email or a cloud drive you control). We never receive a copy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device using the operating system's notification service. No notification content or scheduling data is sent to us.

Third-party services

Yodda does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. No data about your usage of the app is collected by us or shared with third parties.

Permissions

The app may request notification permission (for review reminders) and, when you use export/import, access to share or pick a file. These are used only for the features described above.

Children's privacy

Since Yodda collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's Google Play store page.
